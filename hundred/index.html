what the live brand deals look like

  • Superpower already ran SoulCycle (their first brand deal, Fall 2025), a Yes Theory 20/20 challenge with a $10k prize, Thatch at $179, and employee pages for Workday, Intuit, LinkedIn, Stripe. Yes Theory page.
  • Function does retail and boxes, not gyms: GNC, FabFitFun, Function for Business (500+). Hiring against Aon / Mercer / WTW.
  • Hundred has Cymbiotika ($49 intro) and CAL FIRE Local 2881. Those two motions Superpower has not copied.
  • Mito is practitioner-only on the public site. The partner is the coach, who owns billing.
  • InsideTracker is coach-dashboard. Client results flow to the trainer. Oldest play in the category.
  • SiPhox already built creator landing pages (Ben Greenfield, Nick Norwitz, Cristy Nickels).

how to use this

  • The $50 vs $100 gap is the first thing a mid-tier creator sees. Same software, half the bounty.
  • Do not treat Function as the affiliate fight. Treat them as the distribution fight (retail, box, brokers, Huberman).
  • Mito and InsideTracker are the coach channel. Different offer: cost-plus labs, or a dashboard, not a $50 link.
  • SoulCycle is taken. Barry’s, padel, Othership are the open version of Superpower’s studio play.
  • Keep the two proofs Superpower still does not have: unions, and Cymbiotika-class brand intros.
