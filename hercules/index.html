01 structure

four recruiting lanes. inbound on the side. off-menu for the exceptions.

these are the lanes we recruit against. inbound runs alongside all of them.

lane 01

b2b affiliates

operators and sites talking to people who need software for a real business.

lane 02

creators

educators and builders who teach people to ship. not every ai-tools reviewer.

lane 03

agencies

shops already selling internal tools and mvps. hercules becomes how they deliver.

lane 04

channel distribution

platforms and software next to the operator. negotiated deals, co-marketing.

off-menu

vip · custom deals

a handful of high-leverage partners. custom terms, named owner.

off-menu

zero · vcs + accelerators

no commission. the portfolio company gets the perk instead.

sample of 100. 20 names in each lane. companies only. no contacts yet. filtered to people who already talk to hercules buyers. open the sheet

02 where we start

inbound and the customer list first. then outbound.

200,000 users is the asset. we filter and segment that base, plus whoever already applied inbound, and build the first partners from people who already know hercules. that list will create more opportunity than a cold dream 100. then we go outbound by lane.

first · inward. inbound applicants. the customer list, filtered and segmented. founding-partner invites. an internal campaign so happy accounts can opt in.

then · outbound. agencies, creators, b2b affiliates, channel. not all four at once.

03 week 1

audit. design. foundation. then we are recruiting.

audit
lay of the land. current affiliates, the customer list, paid CAC, conversion, the public page.
design
lock the offer. first pitch below. confirm against margin, then recruit against it.
foundation
the partner assets. tracking, terms, apply flow, billing, first invite, first list.
